How it is made

  1. Base shell: the whole coat is a quilted base (shell + lining, sewn-through). Front panels: 12 cm horizontal channels down to line HL, then chevron quilt lines running from the side seam down to the storm flap (leaf shapes, sewn-through, no loose pods on the front). The back, sides and sleeves are covered by petal pods; their tips show at the side seams in the front view.
  2. Petal pods: each pod is a separate filled leaf shape (shell both faces, filled, closed). It is sewn to the base ONLY along its top curve (the upper 40 % of its edge, see the detail). The lower tip floats free. Rows overlap like roof tiles: each row covers the row below by PO, so rain runs off.
  3. Sewing order for pods: start at the hem row and work up, so each new row covers the attach seam of the row below. Back (first row = 2 leaves directly under the collar, either side of CB), then sides, then sleeves, then the two shoulder wings last.
  4. Shoulder wings: one small pointed leaf (≈19 × 8.5 cm at M) at each shoulder point, attached at its root on the shoulder seam, tip pointing outward almost horizontally.
  5. Closure: METAL #5 two-way zip (gunmetal) at centre front, fully covered by an 8 cm storm flap that runs the FULL length from the collar to the hem, with a brushed-tricot zip guard and chin guard behind it. The flap closes with 9 gunmetal snaps at spacing SN, plus 1 on the collar tab. No zip teeth visible on the outside when closed.
  6. Collar: 11 cm funnel stand, 2 channels, faced with brushed tricot inside; the collar tab extends from the flap and snaps closed.
  7. Pockets: two side-entry hand pockets with METAL #5 gunmetal zips set in the front panel near the side seam (opening PK, top at PP), lined bags in brushed tricot. One inside chest pocket with a METAL #3 zip on the left lining. All sliders carry the ANIEZE leaf puller.
  8. Cuffs: the sleeve is gathered into a 6 cm outer cuff band; an inner rib storm cuff is sewn inside the band.
  9. Hem: clean, no drawcord. The two-way zip opens from the bottom for walking room.
  10. Seam allowance 1 cm; channel stitching 3 mm stitch length; bartack the pocket ends and the top of each pod attach seam.
